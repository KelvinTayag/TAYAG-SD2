Zanka Nijiku

Gatchiakuta Character

Zanka was born into the prestigious Nijiku Family and was expected to become a Hell Guard like his older sister and brother. To fulfill this expectation, he enrolled in the Central Hell Guard Training Academy in the Kamuatari District as a trainee, where he quickly rose to the top of his class. However, his dominance was short-lived when another trainee, Hyo, joined and surpassed his abilities. Zanka realized that he couldn’t hope to compare to a genius like her. Disheartened, Zanka ran away and met Enjin, who inspired him to become a Cleaner and become strong enough to surpass geniuses.

Physical Prowess

Zanka is considered one of the best, if not the best, handlers of a Vital Instrument within the Cleaners, noted for his deep understanding of his tool.

Durability

He shows great determination and physical resilience, continuing to fight even while poisoned or injured.

High Battle IQ

Zanka excels at analyzing enemy tactics, using strategic maneuvers, and utilizing his surroundings to overcome difficult situations.

Relationships

RUDO

Zanka's initial impression of his mentee Rudo was negative, believing they did not connect well. Their first fight confirmed this notion. However, during a conflict with the Trash Raiders, Zanka discovered the conditions for Rudo's Vital Instrument to manifest, fulfilling his mentoring duties and suggesting a potential improvement in their relationship.

ENJIN

Zanka really respects Enjin. When he first met Rudo, Zanka seemed arrogant and overbearing, but he's proud to have been chosen by Enjin to be Rudo's mentor. He's really happy about the recognition from Enjin. Even when Zanka's in a bad mood, a few kind words from Enjin are all it takes to make him smile, and he's actually pretty lovable.

RIYO

Zanka and Riyo are on the same team, but they don't really interact much in the story. Riyo's been with the Cleaners for a while, and she and Enjin are all for keeping the relationship between Rudo and Zanka on track.
